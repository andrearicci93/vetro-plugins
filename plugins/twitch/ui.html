Twitch enhancements

Open Twitch with at least one feature enabled. Vetro starts a protected local session and applies the selected features. Opening Twitch from Samsung Home may cause one restart to prepare the session.

Options

The indicator appears briefly in the top-right corner. It requires ad filtering and is off by default.

Back can exit a stream with the controls visible, and OK can activate a hidden button. With this option, Back hides visible controls and OK only reveals hidden controls.

Waits a random 3–12 seconds after detecting a bonus, then checks it is still available before claiming. Leaving the stream cancels the pending claim.

On the Twitch home screen, select your profile next to Settings to switch accounts. Adding an account uses Twitch’s normal sign-in. Saved sessions are encrypted on this TV. Switching reloads Twitch. Forget saved accounts before uninstalling the plugin if you want to erase them.
